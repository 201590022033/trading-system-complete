# HR9 Adaptive Technical Ensemble — Final Research Report

## Decision

HR9's research engineering acceptance criteria are complete. Adaptive behavior
remains **shadow-only**. The evidence does not support replacing or modifying
the legacy signal, wiring the ensemble into Flask, or promoting recommendations.

## Baselines

- **Pre-HR9 adaptive:** the untouched 101,536-row
  `adaptive_technical_decisions.csv`, version `adaptive-technical-ensemble-v1`,
  SHA-256 `82a4e535ea1d12b122bd2c8657b7d32735c84803e4a019c375c14a7b3eec5121`.
- **Legacy raw:** the unchanged `raw_technical_score` frozen in HR7.
- **Static expanded:** the equal-weight mean of available authoritative signals.
- **HR9 adaptive:** version 2, using shared definitions and causal HR8 reliability
  accumulation with originating signal-state turnover costs.

The pre-HR9 artifact was independently regenerated byte-for-byte before HR9;
its 47,776,297 bytes and SHA-256 matched exactly.

## What HR9 built

- `technical_signals.py`: the authoritative `technical-signals-v1` definitions
  for RSI, SMA, breakout, stochastic, MACD, Bollinger mean reversion, ADX/DMI
  and Ichimoku direction.
- A corrected HR8 walk-forward cost contract: 10 bps per unit of signal-state
  turnover, not a flat charge on every active observation.
- `adaptive-technical-ensemble-v2`: separate 1/3/5/20-session research targets,
  per-indicator evidence counts, bounded weights, contributions, versions,
  hashes and explicit shadow metadata.
- A causal evaluation comparing legacy raw, static expanded, pre-HR9 adaptive
  and HR9 adaptive under 0, 10 and 25 bps cost scenarios.

The new artifact contains 101,536 unique instrument/date/horizon decisions.

## Horizon semantics

| Sessions | Role | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `next_session_tactical` | outcome at the next eligible session |
| 3 | `three_session_short_term` | independent three-session target |
| 5 | `five_session_swing` | independent swing target |
| 20 | `twenty_session_position` | independent position target |

These rows are not votes and are not collapsed into one executable action.

## Causality and integrity

All 101,536 pre-HR9 and HR9 decision keys align. There are zero duplicate keys,
event/availability/decision clock violations, sample-gate violations and
non-shadow rows. Future-mutation tests prove that later close changes cannot
alter earlier scores or weights. Weights remain within 0.5–1.5 and do not move
before 30 eligible observations in their instrument/regime/volatility cell.

overlap for multi-session horizons, so net returns and drawdown are decision

At 10 bps, HR9 adaptive beats static expanded in 30/40 instrument-horizon
cells, but beats legacy raw in only 9/40 and beats pre-HR9 adaptive in 23/40.
Its mean cell advantage is +0.02368% versus static expanded, -0.02697% versus
legacy raw and -0.00117% versus pre-HR9 adaptive.

The late-half 20-session HR9 result is positive (+0.18571% mean net per decision
row; 53.01% active win rate), but the other late-half horizons remain negative.
That isolated result is not stable enough to support promotion. Increasing costs
to 25 bps does not reverse the overall conclusion.

Corrected definitions and costs changed 2,393 actions (2.36%) relative to the
pre-HR9 artifact. This confirms that the corrections are material, but not that
the resulting forecasts are superior.

## Recommendation

Keep HR9 shadow-only. The recommended next milestone is **HR10 — robustness and
admission gates**: purged/embargoed temporal validation, non-overlapping portfolio
simulation, parameter and cost sensitivity, multiple-testing control, per-cell
uncertainty/stability admission, and an explicit reject/continue-shadow gate.
Do not connect the Flask dashboard until that milestone provides a stable,
versioned read-only snapshot contract.
