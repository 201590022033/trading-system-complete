# Shadow and research learning boundaries

Reviewed 5 October 2026. The earlier repaired shadow chain is preserved in [the historical guide](../history/SHADOW_LEARNING_PRE_2026-10-05.md). Its statement that Railway had not been deployed applied to that repair milestone, not the current deployment.

The current Railway worker uses the canonical daily paper workflow plus bounded research lanes. The old selected-only shadow v1 path remains provenance and does not supply default ranking evidence. New canonical paper outcomes retain profile version and horizon; the M11 contextual learner requires mature causal samples and does not silently combine incompatible cohorts.

Daily selected-versus-unselected comparisons remain descriptive. Swing 1.1.0 technical labels, 1.2.0 policy replay, 1.3.0 cloud hypotheses and weekly-news archive observations have different meanings and retain separate attribution. None fine-tunes LLM weights or automatically changes canonical ranking.

Observation, decision, later outcome and contribution are separate immutable events. A target reached only after a later supplied session cannot be known at decision time. Missing causal prices remain unavailable. Durable retries recover only eligible failed/expired jobs and preserve identity; they do not invent another observation or count a duplicate contribution.

Local data and news receipts/outboxes are stored on the home PC; shared research and paper state live in PostgreSQL. The web service is online, while the bounded scheduled worker exits after daily work. See [the actual research loop](../SWING_RESEARCH_LOOP.md) and [current state](../CURRENT_STATE.md).

The first cloud comparison has zero closed holdout samples, and the first accepted local news cases have zero corroborated flags. Prospective walk-forward evidence, independent sources, actual costs and sufficient samples remain required before promotion. Historic SENS research, sector controls and six-family local backtests are future milestones.
