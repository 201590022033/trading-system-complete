# R9 prospective price evidence and accepted research risk

6 October 2026. The local OST and IRESS import paths now preserve each accepted source capture separately under ignored `runtime/local-swing-data/prospective-price-captures/`. A capture records instrument, provider, actual acquisition time, raw file SHA-256 and the source's bounded completed daily bars. Reimporting the same receipt is idempotent. The local `/api/v1/ost/onboarding` status exposes source-specific capture counts, later sessions and changed bars. It does not expose account credentials or browser state. Imported charts continue to feed their existing paths without field splicing.

These captures answer a narrower question than historical B5: what did *we* actually observe, and did a later capture change it? Historical rows in the first export are comparison context, not proof they were available at their session close. A prospective outcome can be assessed only after later completed sessions were independently captured. The status requires at least two captures and four later observed sessions before even flagging outcome readiness. This readiness flag is not a trading signal, performance result or source admission. Existing Railway frozen decisions and their 3/4/5-session labels remain separate.

Research risk accepted for this shadow record: raw/adjusted price basis, volume eligibility, vendor corrections and corporate-action treatment remain unverified. Mitigation is to keep entire providers separate, retain original raw export hashes and all dated captures, count revisions, reconcile independent OST/IRESS sessions when available, check issuer/JSE corporate-action dates, and exclude missing or invalid OHLC from any later label calculation. Volume is descriptive, not proof of executable liquidity. We will proceed with prospective observation without representing vendor semantics as resolved. B5 historical backtest admission, automatic strategy promotion and live trading remain blocked.

The next useful evidence is several successive daily OST and IRESS captures around completed sessions, preferably including an event date. Existing local exports predate this ledger and are deliberately not backfilled as prospective captures. A future milestone can produce cohort outcomes with source-specific as-of checks, costs and sample uncertainty once enough real later sessions exist.
