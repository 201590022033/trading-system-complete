# Sector Forensics

Each represented profile has only one ticker, so ticker and sector effects are
confounded. No sector-level conclusion qualifies as strong evidence.

- **Energy/Sasol:** 3/5/20-session adaptive net means were positive; 5 sessions
  ranked highly, but its first half was slightly negative and no Brent/Rand data
  entered the model. This is a technical-horizon Tier C watch item, not evidence
  for the Sasol macro profile. After the comparator audit it remains Tier C because
  reconstructed adaptive is identical to the raw-technical benchmark.
- **Banks/Absa:** 3/5/20 sessions were positive, but 5/20 reversed sign between
  temporal halves. No rate, bond-yield, growth or Rand observations exist.
  Promising but unproven.
- **Diversified mining/BHP:** 1 and 3 sessions were positive; 5 and 20 were
  negative. Only the 1-session result was positive in both halves. Commodity,
  China-cycle and Rand mechanisms are unobserved.
- **Retail/Shoprite:** only 20 sessions was positive; all shorter horizons were
  negative. No consumer, rate, fuel, inflation or Rand series exists.
- **Offshore earner/Naspers:** all horizons were negative, worsening toward 20
  sessions. The current technical trigger is a reject candidate for this sample.
- **PGM mining/Impala:** all horizons were negative; 1/3/5 were the three most
  damaging areas. PGM, Rand and risk interactions were not measured.
- **Gold, agriculture, index derivatives and USD/ZAR:** no evaluated ticker;
  `INSUFFICIENT DATA`.

The strongest sector priority is not to encode new coefficients. It is to add a
second ticker per sector and time-aligned causal context before retesting the
unchanged hypotheses.
