# ADR 0056 - Multi-source price-resolution workflow

Accepted, 6 October 2026. OST remains the chosen primary JSE research source. Its price-history exports omit Open. The previously captured ViewPoint/IRESS `SOL.JSE` daily table supplies actual Open, High, Low, Close and Volume for 250 Sasol sessions through 5 October, while saved Yahoo Sasol has 19 invalid OHLC rows. Borrowing only IRESS Open would produce an unverified mixed-provider bar; the alternative must be evaluated as a complete independent series.

R6 adds a local read-only source-resolution layer. A bounded, explicit IRESS daily export is registered under an exact instrument/origin identity, raw SHA256, acquisition receipt and retained raw bytes. The audit checks whole-source OHLC, recency, symbol identity, overlapping OST closes and independently reports high/low and volume differences. Saved pre-cutover Yahoo charts are checked for OHLC quality as historical comparison, never fetched as fallback. ShareData and JSE are listed as candidate routes with explicit entitlement/format states; SharePoint is a file store, not a market-data provider. Local dashboard refresh computes per-stock next actions, and the existing daily collector saves the same audit before uploading the unchanged OST v3 dataset.

No alternative chart is spliced into or replaces the primary OST dataset, uploaded to Railway, used for paper decisions, or granted historical AI/backtest admission. Source-action adjustment, volume and historical availability definitions remain open. The old IRESS capture day is documented; its file timestamp is a provisional acquisition receipt, not proof that all historical observations were available then. Future source promotion requires a separate contract and source-consistent evaluation.
