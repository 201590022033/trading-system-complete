# ADR 0059 — Prospective source captures without historical promotion

Date: 6 October 2026. Status: Accepted.

Decision: retain immutable local, source-specific snapshots at each validated OST/IRESS import. Count later observed sessions and revisions for prospective research. Keep historical admission and execution gates unchanged while provider price/volume semantics are unresolved.

Reason: a bank response is uncertain and a current export cannot establish what was available at past decision times. Repeated dated captures can establish our own as-of record and reveal later corrections without inventing historical availability. The price basis, volume eligibility and corporate-action treatment remain explicit research risks.

Consequences: local disk use grows with new unique exports; captures use the existing bounded 600-bar source representation and are excluded from Git. No old export is backfilled into the capture ledger. Prospective outcome analysis requires later independent sessions and separate quality checks. No credential handling, browser automation, historical B5 admission or broker order path changes.
