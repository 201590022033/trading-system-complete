# ADR 0045 — Refine Swing rules through isolated, sector-aware experiments

Date: 2026-10-05. Status: Accepted research-design boundary; numerical candidates remain unvalidated.

## Context

The owner requested richer entry/trailing/exit hypotheses, an adaptive increased-volume contribution and measured sector/share momentum personalities. Reviewed South African research is mixed and often uses indices or month-scale horizons; it does not establish a best 3–5-day technical indicator per sector.

## Decision

Document bounded continuation, reclaim and recovery setups; compare fixed, delayed ATR and delayed structural stops with independent 3/4/5-session exits. Maintain causal next-bar activation and conservative daily path handling. Separate activity flags from predictive volume weights and from risk/liquidity constraints.

Fit future sector/share effects locally with training-only normalization and pooled/shrunk estimates. Require chronological nested tests, independent episodes, cost sensitivity and a prospective shadow stage. Add volume/indicator ablations and preregister the trial budget instead of adding unlimited correlated indicator votes.

## Consequences

The [proposal](../research/SWING_RULE_REFINEMENT.md) becomes the 0.2 refinement of the six-family concept. It neither replaces frozen profile versions nor implements a new backtester/radar or deploys parameters. Future implementation must reuse the existing causal/effectiveness/HR11 and ledger contracts. Canonical ranking, M14/M15, hosted schedules and broker permissions are unchanged.
