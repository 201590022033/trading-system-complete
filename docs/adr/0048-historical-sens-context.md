Date: 5 October 2026. Status: accepted for local research only.

Display times interpreted as SAST are reconstructed research assumptions, not point-in-time proof. No action adjustment, sentiment scoring, trade behavior or price admission is changed.

Use public normal site transports or owner-permitted snapshots, without cookies, credential exports, hidden paywall text or guessed subscriber endpoints.
