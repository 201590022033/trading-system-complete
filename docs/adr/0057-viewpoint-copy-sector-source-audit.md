# ADR 0057 — ViewPoint copied sector histories remain independent candidates

Accepted, 6 October 2026. R7 verified that the signed-in ViewPoint daily chart table supplies complete OHLCV for Sasol and the four registered sector ETFs. The Copy action emits CSV whose timestamp is 22:00 UTC on the previous calendar date for the JSE session. The existing bounded IRESS parser now accepts this observed format, converts to SAST, excludes incomplete sessions, and preserves exact raw-byte receipts. This extends the existing local candidate workflow rather than creating a new feed or authentication path.

Each of the five 250-session IRESS histories has 250 matching OST closes. Every history has at least one high/low difference; three have volume differences. Neither the ViewPoint controls nor the located official product documents define price adjustment, eligible volume, historical revisions or point-in-time availability. Therefore all five remain local whole-source candidates. No IRESS field fills an OST bar, no IRESS candidate is uploaded as primary, and no historical AI or B5 real-data gate changes. A documented provider contract and a corporate-action reconciliation are required before reconsidering promotion.
